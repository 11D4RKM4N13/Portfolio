## Context

Professional project developed at **GSD Plus SAS** for the public transport system of Cartagena, Colombia.

## What I did

- Designed and built APIs for processing **closed-loop** and **open-loop** card transactions.
- Deployed them to production, where they handle **over 10 million transactions a year**.
- Applied the security standards required by transactional systems.

## Impact

A critical production system that serves the city's public transport riders every day.
